_RakshakAI 🛡️ – Advanced AI Safety & Legal Assistant
RakshakAI is a high-performance Android safety app that delivers real-time legal intelligence and multi-trigger emergency response. It combines NLP-driven legal mapping with automated evidence protection to create a 360° personal safety net.

✨ Features
⚖️ AI-Driven Legal Mapping (BNS)
Vector Space NLP Engine: Maps user-described situations to the correct Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) sections using TF‑IDF + Cosine Similarity.

45,000+ Case Dataset: Pre-indexed dataset for high-precision legal retrieval and contextual grounding.

94% Accuracy: Highly accurate situational mapping with a 65% confidence threshold warning system to flag uncertain matches.

Contextual Suggestions: Provides immediate, category-specific guidance (e.g., Cyber, Stalking, Theft) based on the mapped offence.

🚨 Multi-Trigger SOS System
Scream Detection: AI-monitored microphone triggers SOS on high-amplitude scream patterns.

Shake-to-Alert: Accelerometer-based trigger for emergency situations.

Power Button SOS: 4-click hardware trigger that works even when the screen is locked.

Giant SOS Button: One-tap emergency activation from the home screen.

📦 Automated Evidence Protection
Cloud Evidence Sync: Automatically records 2 minutes of audio during an emergency and uploads it to Firebase Storage.

Live Location Tracking: Sends automated SMS with Google Maps live location to emergency contacts.

Companion Mode: Real-time monitoring for late-night travels or high-risk commutes.

🛡️ Proactive Safety Tools
Fake Call Scheduler: Simulate incoming calls (e.g., from “Papa” or “Police”) to exit uncomfortable situations.

Nearby Police Finder: One-tap navigation to the nearest police station.

🧠 Technical Stack
Language: Kotlin

Architecture: MVVM with Foreground Service

AI/NLP: Custom Vector Space Model (TF‑IDF + Cosine Similarity)

Backend: Firebase (Authentication & Storage)

Dataset: 45,000+ entries (CSV & JSON)_
